Extra Virgin Olive Oil

500 ml · Single Origin · Zgharta, North Lebanon

$24.00

This Oil is the stuff of the land. Grown on the same sun-drenched plain where Phoenicians once pressed olives and shipped them across the Mediterranean. Picked by hand at early harvest. Cold-pressed the same day.

The result is luminous and green—peppery at the back of the throat, bright and grassy on the tongue, with a finish that softens into something almost sweet. This is what Olive Oil is supposed to taste like.

For dipping, drizzling, and finishing. Over labneh, warm bread with za’atar, hummus, grilled halloumi, roasted vegetables, eggs. Use it on everything—generously.

Early harvest · Cold-pressed the same day · Unfiltered · No blending, no additives
