The Land, the Trees, the Family.

Three generations on the Plain of Zgharta

This Oil Is Rooted in a Specific Place.

Zgharta sits on a fertile plain in North Lebanon, between the Jouit and Rashein rivers, eleven kilometres from the Mediterranean. The name comes from the Aramaic word for “fortress”—and for good reason. The people here are fiercely loyal to the land, and the land has repaid them in kind. Olives have been the lifeblood of this plain for centuries.

Our family has been part of that story for three generations. We tend the same gnarled, sun-baked trees our grandparents tended. Every autumn, the harvest brings the whole family together—aunts, uncles, cousins, neighbours—picking by hand, pressing the same day, eating together that night. It’s not a marketing story. It’s just how it works here.

The Terroir

Mineral-rich alluvial soil between two rivers. Long, golden summers and mild winters. A sea breeze from the Mediterranean to the west. These conditions produce olives with naturally high polyphenol content—the compound that gives the Oil its peppery bite and its extraordinary shelf stability.

Early Harvest, by Hand

We pick when the fruit is still green and firm—before it fully ripens. It’s harder work and yields less Oil per kilo, but the flavour is sharper, greener, more alive. No rakes, no mechanical shakers. Just hands, ladders, and the same families who have worked alongside us for decades.

Pressed the Same Day

Olives oxidise fast. Every hour between tree and press costs you flavour, polyphenols, and freshness. Our fruit is cold-pressed within hours of picking. Unfiltered. Unblended. What you taste in the bottle is exactly what came out of the press—nothing more, nothing less.
