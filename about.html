Our Story

A New Chapter Rooted
in Ancient Land.

First generation. Bold about it.

We are the first generation of our family to bottle this Oil. But the land is ancient. The groves of Zgharta have been here for centuries—gnarled, sun-baked trees rooted in mineral-rich soil between the Jouit and Rashein rivers.

Zgharta. From the Aramaic for “fortress.” Eleven kilometres from the Mediterranean, in the proud heart of North Lebanon. The people here are fiercely loyal to the land, and the land has repaid them in kind. Olives have been the lifeblood of this plain for as long as anyone can account for.

We didn’t inherit a brand. We inherited a relationship with a place. Oro Di Haikal is our way of sharing it.

How We Make It

From Grove to Glass

The Terroir

Alluvial soil between two rivers. Long golden summers, mild winters, a sea breeze from the west. These conditions produce olives with naturally high polyphenol content—the compound behind the peppery bite and the Oil’s extraordinary stability.

Early Harvest, by Hand

We pick when the fruit is still green and firm. It yields less Oil per kilo, but the flavour is sharper, greener, more alive. No rakes, no machines. Just hands, ladders, and the families who work alongside us every autumn.

Pressed the Same Day

Olives oxidise fast. Every hour costs you flavour, freshness, and polyphenols. Our fruit is cold-pressed within hours of picking. Unfiltered. Unblended. What you taste is exactly what came out of the press.
