The Place

A Fortress of Olives

Zgharta. From the Aramaic for “fortress.” A sun-drenched plain in North Lebanon, cradled between two rivers, eleven kilometres from the sea.

This is one of the oldest olive-growing regions on earth. The Phoenicians pressed Oil on this coast and carried it across the Mediterranean in round-bottomed ships. They gave the world the olive tree. The groves of Zgharta are a continuation of that ancient, unbroken line.

The Process

Nothing Between the Fruit and the Bottle.

We harvest early—when the olives are still green and firm and the polyphenol content is at its peak. Every olive is picked by hand. The fruit goes to the press within hours.

The result is luminous and green. Peppery at the back of the throat, bright on the tongue, with a finish that softens into something almost sweet. No blending. No refining. No additives.
